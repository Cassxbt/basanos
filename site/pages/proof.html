Outcomes

As a predictor of the contest outcome, the shipped check is indistinguishable from report length: AUROC 0.711 against 0.661, intervals overlapping. That is why it gives a missing-evidence checklist, not a score.

Labels are contest outcomes. A valid bug can lose as a duplicate or a downgrade.

SERV vs Dolphin

As the decider, SERV is not more accurate than the open model, at about nineteen times the per-decision cost. Its job here is the guard, the bounded call and the receipt.

Both deciders run on the same evidence in every run, so this is a like-for-like comparison.
